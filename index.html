ABOUT
About

Close to the screen. Rarely close to done.

I'm a Computer Science & Game Design graduate from UC Santa Cruz. Growing up, I was fascinated by how things worked, the mechanics behind them and the artistry in how they came together. That curiosity followed me into software, where coding became its own form of art: building games, tools, and entire experiences out of nothing but logic and imagination.
Most of my hands-on experience comes from games: two multiplayer titles live on Steam, where I've worked on UI systems, gameplay architecture, and performance. I've also branched into data analysis with Python and NumPy, built a TypeScript drawing app from scratch, and I'm now building my first mobile app. I'm a quick learner: once something catches my interest, I don't stop until I've actually gotten good at it, and I bring that same mindset to every project.

Outside of code I draw, cook, and paint nails: creative outlets that keep the same instinct for craft and attention to detail sharp.
